## Description (paste into YouTube)

**Cryptography: Secrets, Locks, and Digital Trust** is a rigorous but accessible 4-hour introduction to cryptography. It is taught, tutored, drilled, and graded by AI, with built-in academic-integrity enforcement.

The curriculum has four hours:

1. **HOUR 1 EVERY CODE EVER BROKEN WAS BROKEN BECAUSE PEOPLE REPEAT THEMSELVES** — Letter counts, stock phrases, weather reports: Enigma fell to habit, not to genius.
2. **HOUR 2 PERFECT SECRECY EXISTS, IS PROVABLE, AND IS WORTHLESS** — Why the unbreakable cipher went unused, and why security is priced, not guaranteed.
3. **HOUR 3 TWO STRANGERS CAN AGREE ON A SECRET WHILE EVERYONE LISTENS** — The one genuinely astonishing idea in the field, and the reason there is an internet.
4. **HOUR 4 NOBODY BREAKS THE MATH — THINGS FALL APART AT THE SEAMS** — Keys, people, sloppy code, and governments: where every real-world break has come from.

Hour 1 explains Linear B: Alice Kober tabulated repeated sign groups, triplets, and changing inflected endings, building a consonant/vowel relation grid without assigning sound values. Michael Ventris then tested Cretan place-name cribs, including Knossos and Amnisos. This was an orphaned script deciphered through repetition and geography, not deliberate concealment; Hours 2–4 examine systems designed to hide information from adversaries.

The course also includes two diagnostic primers. **How to reason about cryptography cases** uses a concrete deployment scenario to identify the architectural idea that decides it. **Core reasoning skills** covers analysis, inference, evaluation, deduction, and induction.

In this video:

- A 4-hour intensive with four topics
- Short, Medium, and Long lesson depths
- A section-scoped AI tutor
- Adaptive practice
- AI-graded homework, a course test, and a final
- Roughly half multiple choice, with most freeform answers taking one or two sentences
- Immediate numeric percentage grading for every question
- Exact text answer persistence and a live answer diagnostic for generated, sent, persisted, and grading evidence
- Text-based and keystroke-pattern AI-authorship detection
- Ungraded cryptography-case and general-reasoning diagnostics

The course is designed for adult researchers, professionals, product teams, engineers, instructors, and academic-integrity researchers.
